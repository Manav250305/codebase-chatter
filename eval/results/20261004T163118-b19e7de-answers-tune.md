> The word that appears in almost every function does not get a negative keyword score because the `PositiveIdfBM25` class uses Lucene's IDF formula, which ensures strictly positive scores for matches. Specifically, the IDF is calculated as `log(1 + (N - n + 0.5) / (n + 0.5))`, where `N` is the total number of documents and `n` is the frequency of the term in the corpus. Even if a word appears in nearly all documents (making `n` close to `N`), the formula still results in a positive value due to the logarithmic transformation and the added constants (0.5), ensuring that matches score > 0 and non-matches score exactly 0 [C1]. This contrasts with the standard Okapi IDF, which can yield zero or negative scores for terms in at least half the documents [C3]. Thus, the positive IDF prevents negative scores for matches, even when a word is common across many functions. [C1, C2]
